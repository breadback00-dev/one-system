# Med Spa Niche Notes

## Why This Niche

- high-value consultations and treatments
- revenue sensitivity to lead response time
- frequent no-show and reactivation opportunities
- strong dependence on reviews and trust
- referral-friendly customer journeys

## Typical Workflows

- new web lead requests consultation
- missed call should trigger immediate text-back
- stale lead receives offer-based reactivation
- recent happy customer receives review request
- existing customer receives referral incentive flow

## Messaging Tone

- premium but warm
- conversion-focused without sounding aggressive
- appointment-oriented
- sensitive to trust, appearance, and discretion

## Early Metrics

- lead response time
- contact rate
- booked consultation rate
- show-up rate
- reactivation booking rate
- review request completion rate
