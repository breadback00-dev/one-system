# Product Spec

## Product Summary

One System is an AI-powered customer acquisition platform for appointment-based businesses.
It combines lead capture, follow-up, reactivation, review generation, referral automation, sales support, and paid lead nurturing in one operating system.

## Strategic Decision

We will build the full five-module platform.
We will not build five isolated products.
We will launch with a med spa implementation first because it offers strong economics, clear workflows, and high-value appointments.

## Core Outcome

We help med spas get more booked consultations and repeat revenue without hiring more staff.

Everything in the platform must tie back to:

- more leads captured
- faster follow-up
- higher booking rate
- higher close rate
- better retention and reactivation
- clearer ROI

## Product Modules

### 1. Lead Capture + Instant Follow-Up

Goal:
Never lose an inbound lead.

Capabilities:

- website lead intake
- chat-triggered lead capture
- missed-call text-back
- instant SMS and email response
- lead qualification flow
- booking handoff

### 2. Database Reactivation Engine

Goal:
Turn dormant leads and inactive customers into booked appointments.

Capabilities:

- CSV import and CRM sync
- segmentation of stale leads and past customers
- AI-assisted reactivation messaging
- offer injection
- booking link follow-through

### 3. Reviews & Referrals Automation

Goal:
Increase trust and generate more inbound opportunities from satisfied clients.

Capabilities:

- review request flows
- satisfaction gating
- AI-assisted review response drafts
- referral campaign sequences
- referral source tracking

### 4. Paid Ads + Lead Nurturing Layer

Goal:
Create more predictable pipeline volume and connect ad spend to outcomes.

Capabilities:

- campaign source attribution
- landing page and form integration
- lead nurture sequences
- ROI and conversion reporting
- ad-channel performance views

### 5. Sales Enablement System

Goal:
Improve consultation conversion and staff performance.

Capabilities:

- call recording ingestion
- transcript storage
- AI scoring and summaries
- objection and script guidance
- performance metrics

## Shared Platform Requirements

These must exist once and be reused across modules:

- accounts and workspaces
- contacts, leads, and customers
- conversations and message history
- bookings and outcomes
- workflow engine
- prompt and template system
- event tracking
- integrations framework
- permissions, settings, and auditability

## Success Criteria

The platform is working when it can:

- ingest a lead
- respond automatically
- schedule or route the lead
- track the outcome
- trigger downstream workflows
- report business results by module and source

## Non-Goals For The Initial Build

- multi-niche polish from day one
- every possible CRM integration at launch
- a fully generalized workflow builder before core flows work
- deep customization before the standard med spa flows are proven
